Everything a term needs, in one place

Stop chasing paper registers and score sheets. Each person enters their own part, and AttendNG does the adding up.

Attendance

Class teachers tick morning and afternoon attendance each day. Weekly and term attendance reports are worked out for you.

Score entry by subject teachers

Each teacher enters CA and exam scores for their own subjects only. Totals and grades are calculated automatically.

Broadsheets

A ranked class broadsheet with positions, grade counts and subject averages, downloaded as a landscape PDF.

Report cards

Professional report cards with your school logo, attendance and teacher remarks, ready to download for the whole class.

Analytics

See how classes and subjects are performing, so you can spot problems early instead of at the end of term.

Separate access for each role

Admins manage the whole school. Teachers only see their own classes and subjects. Your data stays organised and private.
