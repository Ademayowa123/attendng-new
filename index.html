Comprehensive Platform

Everything a term needs, in one place

Stop chasing paper registers and handwritten score sheets. Each teacher enters their own subject, and AttendNG does all the mathematical heavy lifting.

Attendance Tracking

Class teachers tick morning and afternoon attendance in seconds. Automatic weekly totals, averages, and male/female breakdown reports are generated live.

Subject Teacher Score Entry

Each teacher enters CA and exam scores for their assigned subjects only. Totals and letter grades are calculated automatically as they type.

Ranked Broadsheets

Generate a complete ranked class broadsheet with student positions, subject averages, and distinction/credit counts, downloadable as a PDF.

Single-Page Report Cards

Professional report cards with your school logo, attendance statistics, affective traits, and teacher remarks — perfectly fitted to 1 page per student.

Actionable Analytics

Monitor class performance and attendance trends across terms so you can identify struggling students and reward excellence early.

Role-Based Permissions

Admins manage school sessions, classes, and teachers. Subject teachers only access their assigned classes. Your student records remain private and secure.
